## Pipelines

I owned the statistical programming pipeline end to end, from raw data to SDTM, ADaM and TLFs, and
prepared Clinical Study Reports. I built over 800 CDISC-compliant datasets and statistical reports
in SAS and R, supporting FDA submissions for more than 10 pharmaceutical clients.

## Validation

Every dataset was checked with Pinnacle 21 and statistical modeling to find and fix anomalies. I
built a debugging tool that traces each Pinnacle 21 finding back to the patient records in the SDTM
and ADaM datasets. It replaced manual lookups and cut issue resolution time by 75%.

> A finding you can't trace to a record is a finding you can't fix.

## Leading

As a senior analyst I led 12 analysts across concurrent clinical projects, working with
statisticians and data managers. We delivered FDA submission packages within 3 weeks across 15+
studies. One-on-one mentoring on pipeline improvements raised data validation accuracy by 60%.

## Takeaways

Regulated work taught me to check everything and to make every check traceable. I now apply the
same habits to evaluating AI systems.
