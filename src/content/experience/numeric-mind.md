## Leading

The team was 12 analysts across clinical projects that ran at the same time. Leading it meant
setting tasks and timelines, reviewing deliverables, and working with statisticians and data
managers so outputs were accurate and on schedule. One-on-one mentoring on pipeline improvements
cut validation findings by 60%.

## Pipelines

I owned the statistical programming pipeline end to end: raw data to SDTM, ADaM and TLFs, then
the Clinical Study Report. I carried more than 15 studies at the same time and delivered every one
by its deadline, each submission package ready within 3 weeks of database lock.
