## Datasets

I built over 800 CDISC-compliant clinical datasets and statistical reports in SAS and R, supporting
FDA submissions for more than 10 pharmaceutical clients.

## Validation

Every dataset was checked with Pinnacle 21 and statistical modeling to find, fix and report
anomalies. I built a debugging tool that traces each Pinnacle 21 finding back to the patient records
in the SDTM and ADaM datasets. It replaced manual lookups and cut issue resolution time by 75%.

## Takeaways

Regulated work taught me to check everything and to make every check traceable. I now apply the
same habits to evaluating AI systems.
